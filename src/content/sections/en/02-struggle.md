### The Silent Struggle No One Talks About

It’s 6:00 PM. The "witching hour."

You’ve just walked through the door after a long day of work (or maybe you never left). Your patience is already thin, worn down by a thousand emails, traffic, and the mental load of simply existing.

Then it starts.

The whining. The refusal to eat dinner. The negotiation over screen time. The sibling squabbles that escalate from zero to screaming in seconds.

You try to be the "Gentle Parent." You take a deep breath. You get down on their level. You validate their feelings. _"I see you're frustrated..."_

But it doesn't work.

They scream louder. They hit. They ignore you completely.

And then... you snap.

You yell. You threaten to take away the iPad. You use the "scary voice." And immediately, the guilt washes over you.

_“I’m failing them,”_ you think. _“Why can’t I handle this? Why does everyone else on Instagram seem to have this figured out?”_
