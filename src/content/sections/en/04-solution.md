### The 3-Step Protocol

In this workshop, we don't just talk theory. We install a new operating system for your home.

#### 1. The Pause (Regulating Yourself)

Before you can co-regulate your child, you must regulate yourself. We teach you the exact physiological triggers to stop the "Snap" before it happens. You will learn how to lower your own cortisol in 30 seconds.

#### 2. The Boundary (Setting the Wall)

How to state a limit clearly, concisely, and without anger. No more "Okay?" at the end of your sentences. No more 5-minute lectures. Just clear, actionable instructions.

#### 3. The Follow-Through (Holding the Wall)

This is where most parents fail. What do you do when they test the boundary? We give you the "Quiet Consistency" script. It’s boring. It’s repetitive. And it works like magic because it removes the drama that fuels the behavior.

**Imagine your home a week from now.**

Dinner time is... quiet.
Bedtime is... routine.
Tantrums still happen (they're kids, not robots), but they last 2 minutes, not 20. And you navigate them without raising your pulse.

This isn't a fantasy. This is what happens when you stop fighting your child and start leading them.
