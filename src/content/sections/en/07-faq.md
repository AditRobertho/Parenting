  - question: 'Is this workshop suitable for single parents?'
    answer: 'Absolutely. The core principles of Calm Authority apply regardless of family structure. In fact, many single parents find these tools especially empowering as they build a consistent framework for their home.'
  - question: "What if my partner isn't on board?"
    answer: "You can start these changes alone. Often, when one parent shifts from chaos to calm authority, the dynamic changes so positively that the other parent naturally becomes interested. You don't need to wait for perfect alignment to see results."
  - question: 'My child is strong-willed. Will this work?'
    answer: "Yes. Strong-willed children actually crave the safety of 'Calm Authority' the most. They push boundaries to check if they are secure. This method gives them the structure they need without crushing their spirit."
